# USRad Credibility Guidelines

## Overview
This document outlines the strategic approach for mentioning AnciCare and CorVel Corporation across USRad's website to maximize credibility while minimizing legal risk.

## Core Principles

### 1. Strategic Placement Over Repetition
- **ONE** CorVel mention per page maximum
- **TWO** AnciCare mentions per page maximum
- Place mentions where they have maximum impact
- Avoid repetitive name-dropping

### 2. Legal Considerations
- No implied current endorsement or partnership
- Factual historical statements only
- Include appropriate disclaimers where necessary
- Avoid trademark misuse

### 3. Professional Positioning
- Focus on USRad's achievements, not past company names
- Let the acquisition validate without overshadowing
- Maintain clear USRad brand identity

## Page-Specific Guidelines

### Critical Pages (Highest Sensitivity)

#### Employer Page (`/employer`)
- **Special Consideration**: CorVel dominates this market
- **Strategy**: Bury CorVel mention deep in content
- **Placement**: Middle of success timeline only
- **Language**: "Successful healthcare exit" dominates

#### Imaging Center Pages (`/imaging-center/*`)
- **Volume Correction**: $240M not $400M
- **Strategy**: Hero banner for immediate credibility
- **Focus**: Recruitment and partnership benefits

#### Managed Care Page (`/managed-care`)
- **Strategy**: Single mention after comparison table
- **Purpose**: Validate after showing USRad advantages
- **Focus**: True managed care vs booking platforms

### Standard Pages

#### Homepage
- **Placement**: Hero banner recommended
- **Format**: "Acquired by CorVel (NYSE: CVL)"
- **Purpose**: Immediate credibility for first-time visitors

#### About Page
- **Primary Mention**: Hero subtitle
- **Secondary**: Timeline context
- **Include**: Clear disclaimer about independence

#### Other Pages
- **General Rule**: One strategic mention if relevant
- **Alternative**: Use achievement-focused language

## Approved Alternative Phrases

### Instead of "CorVel"
- "NYSE-listed company"
- "Successful healthcare exit"
- "Publicly-traded acquisition"
- "Enterprise validation"
- "Fortune 500 acquisition"

### Instead of "AnciCare"
- "Our previous venture"
- "Our proven model"
- "Our established approach"
- "Through our experience"
- "Our track record"

### Achievement-Focused Language
- "400K+ patients served"
- "$240M+ volume delivered to imaging centers"
- "$400M+ total savings (medical + indemnity)"
- "1,200+ centers recruited"
- "8 years building (1994-2002)"

## Best Practices

### DO:
- ✅ Use factual, verifiable statements
- ✅ Include NYSE ticker (CVL) with CorVel mentions
- ✅ Focus on what the acquisition represents
- ✅ Vary language to avoid repetition
- ✅ Consider page audience sensitivity

### DON'T:
- ❌ Imply current relationship with CorVel
- ❌ Overuse company names
- ❌ Make unverifiable claims
- ❌ Use CorVel logo or branded materials
- ❌ Suggest CorVel endorsement of USRad

## Special Considerations by Audience

### For Employers
- Extra sensitivity due to CorVel market presence
- Focus on "successful exit" language
- Emphasize USRad innovation and independence

### For Imaging Centers
- Can be slightly more direct
- Focus on proven recruitment success
- Emphasize partnership benefits

### For Patients
- Less sensitive audience
- Can use for trust-building
- Keep simple and clear

```
Through AnciCare (1994-2002), we revolutionized medical imaging access. 
Our success led to acquisition by CorVel Corporation (NYSE: CVL),

Now we're bringing the CorVel-validated AnciCare approach to USRad."
```
