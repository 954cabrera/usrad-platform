# USRad Credibility Guidelines

## Overview
This document outlines the strategic approach for mentioning AnciCare and CorVel Corporation across USRad's website to maximize credibility while minimizing legal risk.

## Core Principles

### 1. Strategic Placement Over Repetition
- **ONE** CorVel mention per page maximum
- **TWO** AnciCare mentions per page maximum
- Place mentions where they have maximum impact
- Avoid repetitive name-dropping

### 2. Legal Considerations
- No implied current endorsement or partnership
- Factual historical statements only
- Include appropriate disclaimers where necessary
- Avoid trademark misuse

### 3. Professional Positioning
- Focus on USRad's achievements, not past company names
- Let the acquisition validate without overshadowing
- Maintain clear USRad brand identity

## Approved Alternative Phrases

### Instead of "CorVel"
- "NASDAQ-listed company"
- "Successful healthcare exit"
- "Publicly-traded acquisition"
- "Enterprise validation"
- "Fortune 500 acquisition"

### Instead of "AnciCare"
- "Our previous venture"
- "Our proven model"
- "Our established approach"
- "Through our experience"
- "Our track record"

### Achievement-Focused Language
- "400K+ patients served"
- "1,200+ centers recruited"
- "8 years building (1994-2002)"

> **Volume and savings figures are not prescribed here.** The volume figure is an open
> question — the live provider funnel carries both `$180M+` and `$160M+`, and which is
> correct is unresolved. `$400M+` total savings is withdrawn from approved language.
> Do not substitute another value. Obtain the figure before publishing one.

## Best Practices

### DO:
- ✅ Use factual, verifiable statements
- ✅ Include NASDAQ ticker (CRVL) with CorVel mentions
- ✅ Focus on what the acquisition represents
- ✅ Vary language to avoid repetition
- ✅ Consider page audience sensitivity

### DON'T:
- ❌ Imply current relationship with CorVel
- ❌ Overuse company names
- ❌ Make unverifiable claims
- ❌ Use CorVel logo or branded materials
- ❌ Suggest CorVel endorsement of USRad
