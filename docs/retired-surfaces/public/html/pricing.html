Built by Someone Who's Been There

I've experienced this problem firsthand. After building and selling the first medical imaging network for workers' compensation, I was shocked when I needed an MRI for myself. Hospitals quoted $8,000, but my insurance would only pay their negotiated rate of $1,600 — which became my responsibility because I hadn't met my deductible.

The games hospitals play frustrated me most. If you mention insurance, they won't offer cash prices. But approach them without mentioning insurance, and suddenly cash discounts become available. These games hurt the 90+ million Americans who are uninsured or underinsured.

That's why I created USRad — to eliminate the guesswork, the haggling, and the fear of overpaying. You deserve to know exactly what you'll pay for high-quality medical imaging, backed by a guarantee that ensures your scan meets your doctor's standards.

— USRad Founder
