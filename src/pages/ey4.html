Photo Strategy Guide

🎯 Hero Photos Needed:

  • ✓
    Construction/Labor Workers: Authentic shots of blue-collar workers, hard hats, safety vests
  • ✓
    Medical Imaging Centers: Modern, clean MRI/CT facilities (not hospital-looking)
  • ✓
    Happy Families: Diverse families at home, parents with kids, real moments
  • ✓
    Recovery Moments: Workers back on the job, people active and healthy

📸 Photo Style Guide:

  • •
    Lighting: Natural, bright, optimistic (like One Medical)
  • •
    Color Grading: Warm, approachable tones with blue accents
  • •
    Composition: Wide shots showing environment, close-ups for emotion
  • •
    Avoid: Stock photo look, overly posed, clinical/cold feeling

Pro Tip: Consider hiring a photographer for a half-day shoot at a partner imaging center. Get real patients (with consent) or hire diverse models. Authenticity beats perfection.
