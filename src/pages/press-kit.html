Executive Biography

Michael Cabrera

Co-Founder & CEO, USRad

Michael Cabrera is a healthcare entrepreneur and pioneer in diagnostic imaging network development. In the 1990s, he built one of the first nationwide imaging networks — connecting employers, injured workers, and imaging centers through a direct-contract model that reduced costs and accelerated access to care.

That network served more than 168,000 patients and delivered over $246 million in documented savings before being acquired by a publicly traded company.

At USRad, Cabrera is deploying an evolved version of that same model — this time designed for the tens of millions of Americans who need affordable imaging without relying on traditional insurance systems.

Background: Healthcare entrepreneur with 30+ years of experience building and scaling imaging infrastructure. Selected for MIT's Birthing of Giants program.

About USRad

What We Do: USRad is a diagnostic imaging access platform that connects patients with accredited imaging centers at transparent, pre-negotiated prices — without the complexity of traditional insurance systems.

How It Works: The company operates a direct-contract network model — the same model proven at national scale through AnciCare — now deployed for the direct-to-patient market. Members book online, pay a single all-inclusive price, and receive results within 24–48 hours.

The Market: USRad serves the tens of millions of Americans who need affordable diagnostic imaging and have no insurance coverage to rely on.

Positioning Statement (For Media Use)
